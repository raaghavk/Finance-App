Privacy policy

Last updated 13 September 2026. Operator: Raaghav Kanodia, India. Contact: raaghavkanodia@gmail.com

What Liora stores

Liora is a local-first personal finance app. Your name, accounts, categories, budgets, trips, and transactions are saved in this browser’s localStorage on this device (key zenith_v1_store). We do not require an account to use the app.

If you create a cloud account on You, we store a copy of that same ledger in a dedicated Liora database, keyed to your login, so you can restore it on another phone. You can keep using the app without signing in.

What we do not collect

  • No bank login, UPI credentials, or SMS access.
  • No advertising ID, and no ad or remarketing cookies.
  • We do not sell or share your spend data.

Cookies and local storage

The app uses localStorage for your ledger and a one-time site notice. That is not an advertising cookie. Marketing pages (invite, support, privacy, terms) may record anonymous page views through Vercel so we can see if the public pages load. The in-app ledger is not sent there.

Optional features

Voice parse and receipt OCR only run if those API keys are configured on the server. If you use them, the utterance or image is sent to that provider to return a draft expense, then discarded from our side. You can always type an expense instead.

Liora Pro, if unlocked, is stored as a flag on this device until App Store billing exists.

Your controls

On You, you can export CSV, download a JSON backup, restore a backup, sign in or out of cloud backup, or clear all data on this device. Clearing local data cannot be undone on this phone.

Children

Liora is not directed at children under 13.
